## Course Description

> “Always keep Ithaca in your Mind.To arrive there is your destination.”

Awe touches the Soul as it approaches the ever-flowing spring of Homer, the Source of the Tradition, over which our most ancient forefathers have leaned, generation after generation, for centuries: all those who drew close around the fire of Homer and the Primordial Wisdom recorded, historically, mythologically and mystically, in the great masterworks of the Epics. The eternal Archetypes that tell a primordial story, an age-old journey, familiar and mysterious: the journey of our very own Soul.

We will decode, Philosophically, Psychologically, Experientially and Initiatically, the deeper symbolism and the hidden Wisdom of the Homeric Epic. We will come into contact again with the Ancient Greek Language, with its vibration and its etymology, which has the power to illuminate the Mind and awaken the Soul.

**Odysseus is Us.**

We, the Soul. We, Consciousness.

We, the Human being with the divine spark (or the divine spark in human form!), who journeys through the visible and invisible universes of Matter, of Spirit, of Mind, of relationships, crossing seas between mortal and immortal, oblivion and truth, sleep and Awakening.

It is no accident that as he finally reaches Ithaca, Odysseus is asleep.So he does not, strictly speaking, arrive in Ithaca.Rather, he Wakes Up in Ithaca.

**For Ithaca *Is* Awakening.**The Awakened state of Consciousness that Remembers its true Self.This is its Νόστος, its return home: Remembrance.The State of Presence, of Truth (Α-λήθεια, un-forgetting), the Recognition of Who I Am beyond the dreams of a consciousness asleep.

Homer’s Odyssey, as a primordial Code of the Greek Spirit, carries a unique quality, a hue of its divine nature and origin: it describes at once all the journeys of all our hypostases. It speaks to us of the relation of the One to the Many. The Passage from Unity to Multiplicity. The Journey of the Soul into existence. And the path of our Soul’s Awakening.

For the Odyssey is Life itself, the course of incarnation, and indeed of all incarnations, within the cyclical orbits of the god-made Aeons.

It tells of our very own journey on Earth, through the much-suffering, many-turning human experience.

> ~ “The Laestrygonians and the Cyclopes, wild Poseidon”, whom “you will not meet, unless you carry them within your soul.”

Yet this journey is neither arbitrary nor uncharted. It bears Archetypal Elements, Structure and Stations. The trials we face, the challenges, the passions, the oblivion, are not unique to each of us. They are, archetypally and eternally, the same. The Universe has ways, constants and laws. Even the scenarios of oblivion and the simplicity of un-forgetting are constant.

All of this is mapped in the living guide of the Odyssey, a timeless handbook… for navigating life. Here we meet the distillation of Wisdom. For this handbook, this sacred epic, describes at once, on parallel levels, all the philosophical, theological, physical, historical, ontological and psychological dimensions of life and of our Soul’s journey to the Νόστος of Remembrance, which for Socrates is the very definition of Knowledge.

That is why the Goddess Athena, the Goddess of Knowledge and Wisdom, keeps ever-watchful guard at the side of Odysseus as his guide, handing Wisdom down from generation to generation into the hands of every Odysseus: You and Me, All of us.

**What do the Cyclopes, Circe, the Sirens and Calypso symbolize? What is the deeper meaning of the trials of Odysseus? What is the role of the Goddess Athena? What does Penelope symbolize? What is Ithaca?**

Through Greek Philosophy, Mythology and experiential exercises of Self-knowledge, we will follow the archetypal journey of the Soul to Awakening, with Homer’s handbook as our guide.
