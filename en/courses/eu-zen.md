## Course Description

> “A path of Self-knowledge, Truth, Virtue, Eros and Freedom, whose aim is to return Philosophy to its primordial mission: Awakening, Freedom & the Art of Εὖ Ζῆν.”

At its core lies the ancient Greek ideal of Εὖ Ζῆν, living well. The Cycle of Studies «Εὖ Ζῆν: Philosophy as the Art of Living» is an experiential path of 16 lessons, dedicated to the practical application of Ancient Greek Wisdom in our everyday life, as a living path of Self-knowledge, of the cultivation of Virtue, of reconnection with our Authenticity and of the Awakening of the Soul. We will explore how the timeless Wisdom of the Greeks can illuminate the deepest questions of human existence, guide us through the challenges of everyday life, and transform Philosophy from an object of knowledge into lived experience and a way of life.

Unlike the Cycles that focus on the systematic study of one particular ancient work, here we will travel through selected passages of the Greek Philosophical Tradition and the study of the ancient Greek Language and its etymology, approaching by theme some of the most fundamental and timeless questions of human life.

Through the meeting of philosophical study with personal reflection, dialectic and experiential practice, we will seek to rediscover Philosophy in its original mission:

**Not simply how to learn more, but how to become, every day, That which we truly Are.**

## Indicative Themes

**Unit I: Know Thyself (ΓΝΩΘΙ ΣΑΥΤΟΝ)***The Mystery of the Self and the Awakening of Consciousness*

- What does it mean to Live Truly? What is Eudaimonia?
- What is true Παιδεία, education of the whole person, and what follows from its absence?
- **Who Am I? What are Mind and Consciousness?** Through Plato’s Cave we explore the nature of Mind and Consciousness, the habits and unconscious programs that shape us, in search of Self-knowledge and Awakening.

**Unit II: The Return to Inner Truth***From Self-knowledge to Inner Connection*

- **What is the Soul and what forces govern it? How do I hear my inner voice? How will I find my Οικειοπραγία, the doing of my own work? Why do I feel sorrow?** We delve into the threefold nature of the Soul, into Truth and inner connection, into trauma and Freedom. We seek our Οἰκεῖον Ἔργον, our proper work, and the possibility of our work becoming an ally of our true Nature.
- **What is Virtue? How are the Four Cardinal Virtues cultivated? What does it mean to live Κατὰ Φύσιν, according to Nature? How does Philosophy become everyday practice?** We explore Virtue, the Four Cardinal Virtues, the Aristotelian Mean and living Κατὰ Φύσιν, as the foundations of a life of Truth, Freedom and Eudaimonia.

**Unit III: Eros, Love and Human Relationships***The Other as a Mirror of the Soul*

- What is Eros? Why do we fall in love?
- Conscious partnership: how do we create conscious relationships? Freedom and Love
- How do my family roots and the wounds of the past affect me?

**Unit IV: The Soul in the World***From Knowledge to Virtue, Psychopolitics and Lived Wisdom*

- **Why Is This Happening to Me Now? Justice & Ειμαρμένη: the Greek Counterpart of Karma?** Ancient views of responsibility, retribution and catharsis, in dialogue with the idea of karma. The relation between Fate and Freedom. Is there a deeper order in the events of our lives?
- **Psychopolitics.** How is individual Consciousness connected with collective Consciousness and with Politics? How can we realize our Nature as “political beings”?
