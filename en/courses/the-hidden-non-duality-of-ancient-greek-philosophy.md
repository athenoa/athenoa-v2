# The Hidden Non-Duality of Ancient Greek Philosophy

Each week we will delve into the non-dual teaching expressed by the following great Philosophers ~ Ancient Sages: Plotinus, Plato, Pythagoras, The Pre-Socratics

- With Dr. Athena Despoina Potari
- From January 2027
- Four weekly meetings
- Live on Zoom, recordings for 120 days
- Language: English

## Course Structure & Content

Each week we will explore the non-dual teachings in the work of the following great Philosophers ~ Ancient Sages:

- **Plotinus: Nous and Logos; the One and/ (as) the Self \* *“It is not a View, it is a Union”***
- **Plato: The Hidden non-duality behind the - apparent - Platonic duality \* *One, Eros & Beauty, and Socrates’ secret Female Master***
- **Pythagoras: The Initiate of the Egyptian Pyramids who invented the term “*Philosopher*” \* “*You will realize you were never a mortal*”**
- **The Presocratics: Heraclitus – Parmenides: At the start of the Journey: *En* and *Polla* ~ *The One and the Many***

In our four meetings we will **study** excerpts from key ancient texts (presented by Athena in English translation) in a dialectical way, **decode** their initiatory spiritual etymology, and weave our inquiry with **meditations, contemplation, embodied practices and dialogue**, with a live **QnA** with Athena and our course community.

The discussion will also involve **allusions to mythology**, theology and even poetry and the Arts, as expressions of meaning and teachings pertaining to the holistic non-dual View of Hellenism.
