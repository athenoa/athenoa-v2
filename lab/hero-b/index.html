Benefits of Joining ATHENOA

Would you like to explore deep spiritual wisdom, and the non-dual teachings of key ancient philosophers?

You will walk the philosophical path experientially, with safety and support at every step. You will come into contact with your spiritual roots, and you will receive the proper, holistic Greek Education and the psycho-mental cultivation that they did not give us in schools - and which is now rarely provided by the existing social - educational system.

In an era when over- and misinformation, the proper foundations, the methodical, precise and grounded Education, without “detours” and rhetoric devoid of practical substance, are the elements that characterize the authentic thinker and practitioner of Philosophy.

After many years of research and work, for the first time in modern times, our School introduces a pioneering Study structure based on the Ancient Curriculum of the Platonic Academy - which operated for approximately 1000 consecutive years, from 387 BC - 529 AD!
