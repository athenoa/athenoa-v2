Praxis · chapter 34 · Challenge · 7 days

Thanks, and let it be

Seven evenings: a minute of gratitude, and one ἔστω.

Athena ends lessons with gratitude: εὐχαριστία, from εὖ, well, and χάρις, grace, activates grace, the incalculable factor that completes eudaimonia together with our own effort. For what is hard, Athena offers a mathematician’s tool: ἔστω, “let it be so”, the word a proof begins with. For seven evenings, close the day with a minute of each.

You do
Each evening, a minute of gratitude, then one thing from the day that you let be.
It trains
Gratitude that opens you to grace, and acceptance that loosens resistance.

This practice opens as you walk the path: after lesson 6 of “Living It Now”.
